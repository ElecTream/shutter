# Privacy Policy — Shutter

Effective 26 August 2026.

Shutter is a daily planner published by **ElecTream** (Leeam Sweeney).

## What we collect

- **On-device lists.** Tasks, archive, themes, and reminder times are stored in app preferences on your phone.
- **Google account (if you sign in).** Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication may receive your user id, email, and display name.
- **Photos you pick.** Image picker can access photos you choose (Android storage / media permission).
- **Notifications.** Local reminders use exact alarms and a boot receiver so they reschedule after reboot. A custom reminder sound is bundled in the app.

We do not sell this data. We do not use it for ads.

## Why we need it

Lists and reminders are the product. Sign-in is only used if you choose Google. Photos are only used if you attach one.

## Retention and deletion

Wipe from in-app settings (export/import/wipe). Uninstalling removes on-device preferences. If a Google/Firebase account is linked, request deletion via the Play listing developer contact until in-app account delete ships.

## Permissions (Android)

Internet, notifications, exact alarms (reminders), boot completed, vibrate, wake lock, foreground service (notification actions), full-screen intent, read storage / media (image picker).
